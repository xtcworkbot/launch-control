Import a real launch

Solscan: the token page's DeFi Activities export (export_defi_activities_…csv) goes straight into Trades CSV. Solscan caps exports at 1,000 rows, newest first, so set its time filter to start at the launch. Its Transfers export is token moves, not SOL; leave it out. Other files: trades columns (any order, common aliases accepted): timestamp (unix s/ms or ISO), wallet, side (buy/sell), sol_amount, token_amount, optional slot. Raw 6-decimal token amounts are detected. Holders: timestamp, wallet, balance or pct. Transfers: timestamp or slot, from, to, sol. Plain SOL moves such as funding and withdrawals; include about a day after launch, because withdrawal sweeps often come late. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded. Imports are kept in this browser only.
